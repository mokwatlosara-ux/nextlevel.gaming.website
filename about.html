About Us

Our Story

Founded by Jason Naidoo in 2023, Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was created to bring gaming enthusiasts together in Johannesburg. We cater to individuals, families, schools, gaming clubs and businesses looking for gaming, esports tournaments, birthday celebrations and team-building activities.

Our Mission

To provide an exciting, welcoming and high-quality gaming environment where visitors can play, compete, learn and connect.

Our Vision

To become a leading gaming and esports destination known for innovation, entertainment and memorable experiences.

Our Goals

To encourage esports participation, develop gaming skills, deliver excellent service and create events for all ages.
